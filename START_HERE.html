OPENALGO RESEARCH · PREVIEW 5

From scanner signals
to portfolio research.

The app, VectorBT and Optuna, in one installation.

On Windows

  1. Extract the whole ZIP. Keep the extracted folder somewhere permanent. Do not run setup from inside the ZIP viewer.
  2. Double-click Setup.cmd in that folder. Choose your broker when asked. Setup downloads the required Python tools and dependencies; first setup needs internet and can take several minutes.
  3. Finish in the browser. Create your OpenAlgo account, configure your broker credentials in the app, and open Tools → Backtest & Optimize.

Next time, double-click Start.cmd. Keep the launcher open while using the app. Press Ctrl+C in the launcher to stop the app and research worker together.

On Linux

Open a terminal in the extracted folder and run bash setup-research.sh. Next time, use bash start-research.sh.

Your first test

Upload a scanner CSV, choose your rules and run a backtest. Research reuses OpenAlgo's price archive and obtains missing prices through your connected broker. Start with a small CSV to verify your broker connection.

The Chartink browser extension and NautilusTrader are optional. VectorBT and Optuna are included in the standard setup. Current portfolio support is long NSE cash-equity signals.

Updating an existing installation? Stop it and back up its configuration and data first. Follow the update guide.
