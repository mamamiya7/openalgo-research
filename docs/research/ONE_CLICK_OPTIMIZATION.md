## What the product should do

The user supplies scanner signals, chooses the original trading assumptions, and
clicks **Find stronger settings**. The application decides which supported ideas
are relevant, prepares only their required broker history through OpenAlgo,
spends a declared search budget, and reports whether the evidence supports an
improvement. The answer can be **Keep the original settings** or **Not enough
evidence**. A profitable result is not a required output.

The monetizable work is the research protocol: correct inputs, causal features,
useful conditional hypotheses, resource control, fair comparisons, reproducible
findings and a clear decision journey. VectorBT or Nautilus owns simulation;
Optuna proposes settings; OpenAlgo owns connected-broker history and native
storage. Our layer must not pretend an optimizer chooses the right scientific
question merely because it can maximize a number.

“Comprehensive” means considering the relevant families and explaining admission
decisions. It does not mean testing infinitely many indicators, constructing a
Cartesian product of every setting, or guaranteeing a globally best strategy.

The new `automatic-search-plan-v1` catalog describes these contracts before a

Current automatic exits search profit target, stop distance and the active

Minute holding limits take priority over unused session limits; intraday setups

zero slippage and does not support trailing stops in this adapter.

The original settings are a separate reference, including when decimal rounding
means they are not on a sampled grid. Manual ranges and budgets do not silently
change the automatic preset. The displayed numeric combination count describes
the domain; it is not a count of configurations actually evaluated.

The four market presets are no extra condition, rising Nifty trend, normal Nifty
volatility and high Nifty volatility. They use the recorded descriptive SMA and
volatility recipe, not learned RSI/EMA thresholds. All strategies receive the same

## Dependency order

### 1. Make the intended work inspectable

Implemented foundation: `services/research_automatic_catalog.build_plan` takes a
portfolio and the interval already resolved from its signals. It returns a
deterministic plan identifier, exact execution-recipe version, searchable domains,
fixed assumptions, validation stages, budgets and planned/unavailable families.

Integrated into `services/research_portfolio.preview` after `resolve_inputs`.
It must not run the price planner, request broker data, execute an engine or write
source records. Put the compact summary alongside the existing run action;
details can expand to “Tests included”, “Kept unchanged” and “Not available yet”.
Do not add dozens of mandatory checkboxes or advertise planned indicators as
enabled. Reopening an old result uses its recorded recipe, not today's catalog.

### 2. Reject isolated numerical peaks

The separately versioned exit-v2 slice checks adjacent declared grid steps, one
parameter of one strategy at a time. The deterministic plan covers up to eight
neighbors per finalist, with at most three finalists and two development windows:
48 additional simulations. Report available neighbors, evaluated neighbors and
any truncation. This is a bounded local check, not a complete sensitivity surface.
Choose the subset by balancing strategy, axis and direction counts before any
neighbor score is observed. Otherwise eight strategies could consume the entire
budget testing only a shorter holding period and falsely imply broad stability.

At least one neighbor must exist. At least 75% of planned neighbors must pass the
same support gates in both development windows: five closed trades, five distinct
entry dates, positive return after costs, drawdown at most 25%, and better
return-minus-drawdown than the unchanged strategy. Neighbor outcomes never choose
new parameters and never use final-period prices. Record stable, fragile or
unavailable; instability must not silently count as a passed check.

These are fixed engineering thresholds to validate and calibrate, not a claimed
confidence level. Existing v1 reports, ranges, hashes and resume histories remain
unchanged. Stability validation does not add stock indicators or broader regime
discovery.

### 3. Admit causal stock features before searching them

Build the immutable feature-input contract next. Keep prior observable price
warmup separate from scored scanner opportunities. Carry instrument, exchange,
interval, timestamps, `available_at`, source receipts, missingness and adjustment
basis. Prepare the union for all admitted feature lookbacks and holding bounds
once; individual trials must not request fresher data.

| Family | Meaningful hypotheses | Admission requirements |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stock trend | Direction and distance from SMA/EMA; agreement across horizons | Completed closes; exact initialization/warmup and minimum history |
| Momentum | Positive/negative momentum and bounded magnitude bands | Completed aligned closes; declared return horizon |
| RSI | Strength/weakness or reversion candidates | Pinned smoothing and zero-denominator policy; continuous warmup |
| Relative strength | Stock return relative to a named market or sector index | Explicit index identity, aligned observation times and return basis |
| Bollinger Bands | Breakout or position within bands | Declared window, dispersion estimator and multiplier; no automatic mean-reversion assumption |
| ATR | Volatility-scaled stop/target hypotheses | True observed OHLC plus prior close; smoothing, price units and causal conversion |
| Liquidity | Minimum traded value or participation constraints | Observed volume provenance and units; missing volume is not observed zero |
| Timing | Session-entry clocks or day-specific hypotheses | Actual session hours, minute history and enough distinct dates |
| Filter stacks | Small combinations of individually useful families | Versioned AND/OR and missing-data semantics; explicit complexity cap |
| Portfolio risk | Allocation and exposure alternatives | User-declared risk envelope, concentration and shared-cash accounting |

Start with daily cash-equity price features already supported by native auxiliary
series. Other exchanges, shorts, options, futures, rolls, margin and contract
multipliers require explicit asset profiles and engine capability checks; an
indicator being available in a library is not enough to admit that asset class.

For each signal, features may consume only information available before its
decision. Prefix and future-perturbation tests must prove this. Continuous required
session windows cannot be replaced by “last N observed rows” across missing
sessions. An EMA needs a declared state/initialization contract; silently swapping
SMA and EMA to reduce warmup changes the scientific hypothesis.

Missing prerequisites block that family. If the advertised protocol requires it,
return incomplete or request sufficient data; do not drop the test and label the
remaining result comprehensively verified. Feature rejection and missing-price
exclusion are separate receipt categories.

### 4. Search hypotheses jointly, then refine

Implement `automatic-hypotheses-v2` only after the causal evaluator exists. Each
hypothesis has an identifier, algorithm version, conditional parameters,
data requirements, capability predicate, complexity cost and fixed evaluation
policy. A disabled family has no dummy parameters. Distinct hypotheses with
identical native behavior should reuse evidence, not consume duplicate work.

First compare broad, simple hypotheses using a bounded share of the development
budget. Include no extra filter as a control and representative exit settings
within each admitted family. Then refine promising neighborhoods using new
development proposals; do not limit a family's entire search to exits that won
without that family. Begin with one additional family at a time, and admit small
stacks only after activity, history and complexity checks justify them.

Optuna supports conditional search spaces; its TPE tooling includes grouped
conditional sampling. This makes it a suitable proposal engine, but the pinned
adapter must explicitly implement, validate and checkpoint the new distributions.
Current numeric-only adapter behavior does not acquire conditional support just
because the upstream library offers it. See the official
[Optuna sampler documentation](https://optuna.readthedocs.io/en/stable/reference/samplers/index.html).

Freeze a finite total budget before search. Keep separate counters for proposed,
repeated, infeasible, failed and actually simulated configurations; budget all
references, validation, stress, neighborhood checks and report reconstruction.
Reserve final-check capacity rather than consuming it during exploration.
Wall time, memory, feature preparation and native engine event/matrix bounds need
independent enforcement. Proposed 100–200 search proposals are a calibration
starting point, not an existing entitlement or runtime promise.

Never let the search extend itself until it finds a profitable result. Pruning
must use comparable completed validation observations; the first weak stretch
of a market is not automatically a valid reason to terminate a trading strategy.
Parallel proposal scheduling changes reproducibility requirements and must have
its own recorded policy and deterministic-resume tests.

### 5. Make the finding useful and falsifiable

Keep original scanner opportunities and price-coverage exclusions fixed. Apply
entry conditions to signals and rerun the full shared-account engine. Filtering
a completed trade ledger cannot reproduce cash competition, later opportunities,
fees or exposure.

Chronological development and final periods are allocated from recorded exchange
sessions. Purge every potential holding deadline at boundaries. Warmup can use
earlier observable prices; final outcomes cannot choose features, thresholds,
budgets or parameters. Preserve account-owned date-use reservations across retries
and reuploads. Price warmup cannot invent earlier scanner signals, and software
cannot certify that someone never viewed the dates elsewhere.

Compare against unchanged settings and explicit idle cash. A named index may
provide reporting context, with price-return/total-return basis stated; it is not
automatically the optimization objective or an execution-matched alternative.
Future benchmark-aware ranking needs its own recipe and baseline contract.

Show support across distinct entry dates, development windows, costs, exposure,
concentration and nearby settings. Zero-trade or nearly idle outcomes cannot win
an improvement claim by merely avoiding losses. Treat candidate selection among
many tests as a multiple-comparison problem; do not report nominal confidence
from the winning trial alone. Calibrate any future statistical claim separately.

## Consumer journey

1. **Upload or open a scanner.** Show source dates, signals and instruments.
2. **Find stronger settings.** Automatically resolve the supported tests and price
   interval; show one compact preflight with original risk assumptions.
3. **Prepare prices.** Reuse Historify, download missing broker windows, show
   coverage and actual remaining work. Feature warmup is a separate input scope.
4. **Research.** Show the stage and completed work against its budget. Pausing and
   resuming retain evidence, proposals, checks and reservations.
5. **Review the finding.** Lead with stronger candidate, unchanged original, or
   inconclusive. Show original-versus-candidate, observed conditions, support and
   final check; put the hypothesis inventory and failed gates in details.
6. **Save or backtest exactly.** Preserve selected settings, condition recipe,
   source bytes, price identity and report. New data starts a new research idea
   with inherited exposure history; it does not overwrite the old result.

The default screen should not look like a laboratory configuration panel. It
should make the computer's work and the strength of its conclusion understandable.
Exploratory controls can remain available without becoming prerequisites.

## Acceptance before enabling broader one-click claims

- Catalog domains match native Optuna axes, and the original configuration stays
  unchanged. Unsupported families cannot reach the engine through UI flags.
- New execution versions coexist with exact saved v1 replay and continuation.
- No-lookahead tests perturb future prices, outcomes and final-period data.
- Coverage, warmup, calendar boundaries and engine limits fail with truthful
  reasons. Missing values never become favorable neutral indicators.
- Independent hand-checkable fixtures verify condition admission, fees, shared
  cash, exclusions and candidate/baseline comparisons.
- Cancellation, pause, deduplication, retries, backup/restore and checkpoints
  preserve the same hypothesis plan, evidence and finite remaining budget.
- No-improvement, insufficient-support, blocked-family and exhausted-budget
  results remain usable. The application must not force a recommendation.
- Only implemented, tested and enabled stages are presented as part of the run.


## Research decision engine - proposed architecture, 30 September 2026

This is the next implementation contract, not a claim that these methods run
today. The current catalog explains fixed recipes. The proposed research planner
chooses a relevant, finite research programme before it produces candidate scores.

### The question and the strategy identity

Answer: **Which settings are best supported for this strategy, trading objective,
risk envelope and the market conditions we can actually test?** The objective is
part of the question. Default to a visible balanced preset; version its exact
selection rule and risk limits before execution. Never change it after outcomes.

Distinguish these two research subjects:

- **Supplied opportunities:** a positive-signal CSV supports exits and additional
  entry filters on its recorded opportunities. It says nothing about stocks or
  dates that did not trigger. A new RSI filter is a declared modification.
- **Executable scanner:** optimizing the scanner's own RSI/EMA thresholds requires
  reproducible rules, the point-in-time historical universe including non-triggers,
  every rule's historical inputs, and decision-time semantics. Generate all resulting
  opportunities before simulating. More OHLC for eventual CSV symbols cannot
  recover missing opportunities. Current Chartink capture does not supply this.

Preserve signal-strategy identity separately from hypothesis identity. Optimizing
original scanner rules must not be advertised for the first subject.

### Method registry and applicability

A versioned `MethodSpec` declares family, economic hypothesis, supported assets
and engines, required fields, warmup/initialization, observation availability,
conditional axes and domains, missing-data semantics, incompatibilities, complexity
cost, evidence prerequisites, evaluator version and numerical reference tests.
Only allow-listed implementations execute. A proposed method is not runnable.

Consider every registered method; record `admitted`, `fixed`, `unsupported`,
`insufficient_data`, `redundant` or `deferred_by_budget`. Budget deferral does not
mean tested. An extensible registry is auditable; 'all known quant methods' is not
a finite executable space. The earlier family inventory owns indicator coverage.
Statistical/optimization methods have different jobs:

| Method | Purpose | Admission rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Small grid / space-filling screening | Compare simple hypothesis families | Exhaustive only if the finite space fits the budget; reserve exploration for every admitted family |
| Conditional multivariate TPE | Refine joint filter/exit settings | Branch-specific parameters, no inactive dummy axes, fixed implementation/version |
| Pareto analysis | Show return/risk/activity trade-offs | Alternatives do not replace the frozen recommendation objective |
| Purged chronological / walk-forward tests | Check later-period performance | Recorded sessions, holding-overlap purging, adequate distinct dates; train before validation |
| Block bootstrap and cost/fill stress | Check dependence and execution fragility | Aligned account-return blocks or full resimulation; no independent-trade shuffling that changes shared cash |
| DSR / PBO and selection-bias diagnostics | Assess the effect of searching many candidates | Complete lineage, appropriate candidate-return matrix, declared assumptions and independently calibrated implementation |
| Learned regimes, ML, clustering or GARCH | Test trainable context/prediction hypotheses | Sufficient continuous history; all fitting/transforms inside training; fixed-rule context first |
| Allocation / sizing models | Compare feasible portfolio exposure | User-approved risk envelope, actual contract/margin semantics and estimation uncertainty |

Machine learning and fractional Kelly are gated extensions, not default
requirements for nine months of scanner signals. Credit, rates, VIX, options and
cross-asset claims require those actual named series; stock OHLC cannot manufacture
them. Never run many diagnostics and select whichever approves the winner.

### Executable ResearchPlan compiler

Implement pure `compile_research_plan(context, registry, policy)`. It reads no
candidate scores and starts no downloads. Context carries `ResearchIntent`,
`InputProfile`, source membership/dates, asset/interval profiles, risk limits,
engine capabilities, known input receipts and prior research/date-use lineage.
The immutable `ResearchPlan` records:

- every method-admission decision and the coverage denominator;
- conditional hypothesis graph, original strategy and no-extra-filter control;
- required series, deduplicated history windows and causal feature contracts;
- finite domains, complexity limits and chronological evaluation policy;
- objective, support/risk gates, exact comparison basis and permissible result labels;
- proposal/simulation/feature-memory/wall-time budgets and stopping rules;
- durable identities, cache policy and reserved final-check capacity.

Compile in two phases. The preliminary plan requests required coverage through
Historify/OpenAlgo. The final plan binds actual calendar, availability and frozen
receipts before any candidate score. Changed input creates a new identity. Trials
cannot obtain fresher prices or revise prerequisites to improve their result.

Domains respect asset price/tick constraints, strategy intent and holding bounds.
Any distribution-derived bounds consume development inputs only. Trailing on/off,
fixed versus ATR exits and clock limits are conditional branches once implemented.
Fees, adjustment basis and fills are factual assumptions or stress scenarios,
not knobs adjusted downwards to win. Do not select an engine by whichever reports
higher P&L. Unsupported execution semantics block that branch.

### Plan validation from the information actually available

Use recorded sessions, distinct decision dates, maximum holding overlap and known
coverage before observing outcomes. Choose among tested chronological fold
policies when feasible instead of forcing identical percentages on every strategy.
Keep feature warmup separate. Many stocks on one date are correlated observations.
The current five-date gate is an engineering safeguard, not statistical confidence.

A short-history/long-hold strategy may permit exploration but not a validated
recommendation. Never silently shorten the original hold, drop inconvenient dates,
invent earlier scanner opportunities, or label exposed final dates unseen. More
warmup OHLC does not create more historical signals. Existing v1/v2 recipes retain
their chronology; adaptive planning belongs to a new execution version.

### Adaptive search controller

The bounded worker checkpoints a declared sequence using development data only:

1. Calculate unchanged and no-extra-filter controls with the same shared account,
   price eligibility, costs and comparison basis.
2. Give every admitted simple family a screening allocation. Explore representative
   exits inside each family; exit-first screening can miss condition-dependent winners.
3. Refine promising joint settings through conditional TPE while retaining a
   preregistered exploration reserve. Refit learned features correctly inside folds.
4. Add limited two-family stacks only when history/activity/complexity gates pass.
   Do not form a Cartesian product of every indicator.
5. Eliminate candidates only on comparable completed observations. One bad initial
   market stretch is not sufficient pruning for a strategy spanning other regimes.
6. Stop at the fixed work cap, domain exhaustion or prerecorded development
   improvement rule. Never extend the search until something becomes profitable.

Illustrative allocation: 25% screening, 40% refinement, 25% validation/stress and
10% final/report reserve. These are calibration candidates, not implemented
budgets or time promises. Count proposals, duplicates, failures, infeasibility and
actual simulations separately. Include references, retries and report reconstruction.

Persist proposals before calculation and commit outcomes atomically. Resume the
same plan/evidence and remaining budget. Cache features by full input/algorithm
identity and equivalent simulations by full execution identity, never symbol alone.
The existing durable job/Optuna foundation is reused, not replaced.

### Recommendation controller and research ledger

First enforce feasibility, risk, coverage and activity. Rank development candidates
under the frozen objective; favor supported neighborhoods and simpler alternatives
when the declared comparison cannot distinguish them meaningfully. Keep the
unchanged original eligible. A zero-trade or nearly idle candidate cannot earn an
improvement claim simply by avoiding losing trades.

Retain aligned account-return series and every evaluated/rejected/pruned proposal
under a durable `ResearchLedger`. Include indicator choices, thresholds, objectives,
seeds, combinations and reruns. Repeated or strongly related proposals are not
independent trials. DSR needs return moments, length, dispersion and an explicit
independent-trial assumption; PBO needs the right candidate-by-time matrix and
resampling design. If prerequisites fail, the diagnostic is unavailable, not a
manufactured confidence percentage. A new CSV filename cannot reset exposure.

Challenge finalists on later development windows, costs, supported fills, nearby
settings and dependence on a few stocks/dates. A regime-specific recommendation
requires enough regime observations and a causal regime rule. Naming winning dates
a regime after seeing outcomes is not a valid classifier.

Freeze the development choice before the reserved final evaluation. The final can
corroborate or reject it; it cannot rerank the alternatives. Failure ends that
recommendation. Further work on exposed dates is exploratory unless genuinely
later evidence exists. Bootstrap resampling does not restore an untouched holdout.

Output `Recommendation`: `supported_candidate`, `keep_original`,
`exploratory_only`, `inconclusive` or `blocked`, with exact settings, original
comparison, objective/risk context, tested conditions, support and uncertainty,
method coverage, input/plan identities and next admissible action. Never force a
winner from an unprofitable shortlist.

### One-click UX and rechecking

One action resolves and executes the admitted programme. Lead with recommended
settings, changes from the original, conditions where support was observed, and
what could invalidate the finding. Show a compact original-versus-candidate
comparison. Put a few meaningfully different risk/return alternatives and the full
research inventory under details. Trial tables are supporting evidence.

Recheck when genuinely later history/source is intentionally added, a saved setup
is rerun, or separately enabled monitoring supplies new evidence. Compare with the
incumbent, append evidence and preserve prior recommendations. Method upgrades
create new studies; they do not rewrite old findings. Live monitoring and automatic
orders/position changes remain separate capabilities.

An LLM may explain registered methods or summarize verified report fields. It must
not improvise parameters, invent confidence, choose hypotheses from reserved
outcomes or execute generated trading code. The intelligence is a tested research
decision process, not a text generator declaring which strategy looks best.

### Next implementation gates

1. Executable intent/input/method/plan/recommendation contracts and pure admission
   compiler. Fixtures: EOD, intraday, short history, long holds, missing volume,
   missing benchmark, incompatible engine and positive-only scanner input.
2. Immutable causal feature bundle and daily EMA/momentum evaluators: prefix
   invariance, exact availability, missing sessions and native execution parity.
3. Data-aware chronology, conditional joint search, bounded family allocation,
   deduplication and deterministic pause/restart with unchanged evidence.
4. Candidate-return lineage, stress/stability and calibrated selection diagnostics.
5. Recommendation-led review, exact replay/export, and incumbent rechecking on
   later data. No-edge fixtures must permit no recommendation. Measure false
   discoveries and selection quality, not only successful execution.

Deliver one complete trend/momentum research journey before expanding the registry.
Then admit RSI, relative strength, Bollinger, ATR, liquidity and supported timing
in stages under their reference tests. Broader asset profiles follow actual engine
and data contracts. OpenAlgo owns broker data; VectorBT/Nautilus simulate; Optuna
proposes; our research decision engine chooses relevant tests and presents the answer.
