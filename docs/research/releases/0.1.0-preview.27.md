# OpenAlgo Research 0.1.0-preview.27

Automatic research now records the decisions needed to avoid treating a sparse,
concentrated historical winner as a reliable improvement. New editable runs use
the explicit `automatic_design_v1` policy; old frozen studies keep their exact
recipes and numerical contracts.

## Changes

- Treat the original order size as a reference for new automatic risk sizing.
  Registered half/quarter trade-risk targets may use a larger cash budget within
  the original strategy allocation and unchanged account, symbol, position,
  turnover, liquidity, correlation and stop-risk limits. Reduced sizing applies
  its fraction once. Existing execution-search v1-v5 recipes are unchanged.
- Require at least 30 closed trades, 10 entry dates and participation across two
  of three predeclared complete-entry calendar thirds, with three entry dates
  per active third. Reject more than 70% of trades in one third or more than 50%
  of positive symbol-level profit from one stock. These are recorded historical
  training admission rules, not statistical independence or a confidence claim.
  Later-period validation retains its existing recorded rules.
- Choose calendar bins from actual complete-horizon opportunities before
  candidate gates or outcomes. Start historical prefixes with room for 60
  complete holding-window sessions when available. Preserve the original
  holding rule and full search range; overlapping fits remain historical.
- Keep all 408 proposals and the 512-account ceiling. If no changed setup passes
  the admission and baseline-improvement checks, retain the original settings
  with the headline **No reliable improvement found**.
- Add separate starting-capital planning for the frozen chosen rules. Original
  capital, allocation and order size define an initial rupee ticket; the planning
  account adjusts the order percentage to preserve that initial ticket at each
  tested capital. Later ticket amounts vary with account equity. All risk limits
  remain intact. Up to four historical planning accounts (three when the full
  later-period programme is retained) fit inside the same 512-account ceiling.
- Report the analytical estimate separately from a native-verified sufficient
  amount. Show when no tested amount funded every eligible request and identify
  position, liquidity, whole-share or missing-evidence blockers. This is neither
  mathematically minimum capital nor a forward funding guarantee. It does not
  change the primary search selection, its capital or its reported returns.
- Bind compact support evidence to its actual calendar and native closed trades.
  Recompute saved selected-report checks and planning receipts on reopening.
