New editable automatic studies use a recorded decision policy. The existing
Optuna 5.0 engine learns ordinary support, sample-design and native financial
feasibility constraints. The complete 408-proposal programme and 512-attempt
ceiling are retained. A frozen changed candidate is challenged against higher
costs and deterministic neighboring settings; historical prefixes and seed
repeats remain diagnostics rather than independent validation.

The result now records an explicit verdict and next action. Fragile candidates
can be rejected even when their ordinary historical profit is positive. The
verdict survives standalone selected-report export and exact checkpoint
recovery. Historical evidence is never relabeled as independent validation.

Capital diagnostics distinguish recorded shared-cash shortages from policy,
ticket, liquidity and evidence constraints. They stop automatic increases when
the problem needs a separate decision and do not falsely claim verified funds.
The original allocation contract and all hard risk limits remain unchanged.

Old saved studies retain their existing numerical contracts. New UI submissions
explicitly opt into the policy; previously saved reports stay readable. No new
remote LLM service, credentials or accounting dependency is introduced.
