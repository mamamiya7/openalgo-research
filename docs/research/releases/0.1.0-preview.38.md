# OpenAlgo Research 0.1.0-preview.38

14 November 2026. A focused follow-up to preview.37 on OpenAlgo2.0.3.3.

[Download the installable ZIP](https://github.com/mamamiya7/openalgo-research/releases/download/research-v0.1.0-preview.38/openalgo-research-0.1.0-preview.38.zip).

Renaming a study or completing a newer data preparation could leave its reviewed
plan pointing at an older revision or preparation record. Start correctly refused
that stale plan, but reopening it did not repair the review. Preview.38 refreshes
the pre-Start plan from the current native preparation without downloading again.
The original stale-plan rejection, ownership and exact-data guards remain intact.
Running and completed studies keep their original settings and evidence.

The runtime fix is `6283464f31962d09ce5ac3489b22d863759eccc1`. Relative to
preview.37, no engine, dependency, frontend, capital policy, search allowance or
financial validation code changes. The full408-proposal/512-account programme,
fixed risk limits and exact saved-result checks remain unchanged.

Qualification:26 focused reviewed-journey/exact-save regressions passed. Signed-in
Chrome started a fresh five-stock,68-signal real saved-data case. Native funding
recovery changed capital and ticket size; the configured ChatGPT changed trailing
and holding settings from measured failures, validated those changes and stopped
with a reason when none passed. The outcome and exact tested report saved and
reopened through the library, with367/512 accounts used and zero new accounts on
reopen. This is evidence of the decision flow, not a passing market strategy.

input and lock hash. Preview.37's complete Windows/Linux CI is the prior baseline;
