# OpenAlgo Research 0.1.0-preview.4

A native **Backtest & Optimize** workflow for OpenAlgo, packaged as one source
distribution with a built interface and locked dependencies. This preview targets
OpenAlgo 2.0.2.2 and Python 3.12.

[Download the preview](https://github.com/mamamiya7/openalgo-research/releases/tag/research-v0.1.0-preview.4)
· [Install and start](../RUNTIME.md)
· [Update an installation](../DISTRIBUTION.md#updates-and-recovery)

## Included

- One to eight long NSE cash-equity CSV signal strategies with individual rules,
  allocation caps and a shared-capital account.
- VectorBT 0.28.5 backtests and Optuna 5.0.0 TPE/grid optimization, including an
  optional later-period check and exact selected replay.
- Optional NautilusTrader 1.231.0 through its locked Linux/WSL runtime.
- Automatic daily or one-minute planning from all inputs and optimizer ranges;
  native Historify reuse and missing-only downloads through OpenAlgo's connected
  broker history service.
- Combined results, strategy contributions, trades, trials, saved signals and
  exports in the native interface, plus account-scoped research MCP tools.

## Changes in preview.4

The NSE calendar uses reviewed 2025–2026 entries and plans only the sessions
required by strategy entry and holding rules, including special sessions. Its
targeted update preserves custom calendar entries and exact older reports.
Worker shutdown checkpoints interrupted work. Daily and timed CSV examples are
available in a compact help section.

The native Fyers history adapter now recognizes explicit successful no-data
responses without retrying them, and propagates exhausted failures or malformed
chunks instead of treating incomplete acquisition as success.

## Verification and limits

The final Windows research suite passed **774 tests, with 42 skips** and one
existing Pydantic warning. Frontend checks and build passed. A fresh preview.4 ZIP
installation on an existing Windows host passed account setup, real VectorBT and
Optuna calculations on controlled data, exact replay, restart preservation and
owned-process cleanup, plus **40 artifact checks**. Publication-only documentation
edits followed that artifact check. Earlier Linux fresh-install/reinstall and
real Nautilus boundary checks are recorded in [current status](../STATUS.md).

Real broker evidence covers Fyers daily and minute data, separately from the
controlled installation checks. Docker execution, clean OS installation, broad
cross-version upgrades and all-broker live acceptance remain open. The supported
scope is long NSE cash equities; [connector limits](../CONNECTORS.md) apply.
Research exports do not activate trading strategies.
