## Predeclared challenges

Before any account outcomes, the compiler records these four masks using only
original scanner identities and the final-development search calendar:

| Challenge | Recorded subset |
|---|---|
| Symbol group 1 | Alternating symbols after deterministic SHA256 symbol ordering |
| Symbol group 2 | The complementary original symbols |
| Earlier dates | First half of the recorded final-development sessions |
| Later dates | Remaining final-development sessions |

Each mask binds exact source-row and account-observation identities. Grouping is
independent of returns, trial winners and final-period observations. At least two
original symbols, 20 recorded sessions per challenge and original opportunities
are required. Missing full observation sessions, including sparse minute unions,
are unavailable; artificial daily returns are never inserted.

The mandate gives these periods the `development_sensitivity` role. They overlap
development deliberately and never acquire an untouched-period claim. The final
verification period remains separate and cannot choose settings.

## Actual account execution

Only a non-reference development choice that already passes later-window,
higher-cost, paired-return, measured-neighbour and native-constraint checks
receives these challenges. Earlier failure records **Not run** and avoids
unnecessary calculation. Insufficient mask breadth records **Insufficient**.

For each available challenge, the selected settings and unchanged reference run
as two fresh native accounts on the same frozen mask. This adds at most eight
accounts within the existing ceiling; the conservative call reserve, including
64 lost attempts, is 373 of 384. No proposal count or data acquisition increases.

The ordinary maximum-holding and timing-union purge applies at each date boundary.
Prices, causal features, fees, slippage, starting capital, allocations and limits
are retained. Filtering symbols keeps every strategy definition, including an
empty component: a 50% allocation does not silently become 100%. Cash competition,
rounding, capacity consumption, correlation and exits are recalculated by native
VectorBT. Completed full-account curves or independently filtered winning trades
are never used as substitute subset accounts.

## Recommendation policy

All four candidate/reference pairs must verify their exact planned source and
observations, frozen study binding, settings per role, normal costs, portfolio
features and account assumptions. Each role needs five closed trades and five
closed-trade entry dates. These counts describe support; they do not establish
independent observations.

Every challenge requires positive candidate return after costs, an improvement in
return minus maximum drawdown over its matched unchanged reference, and maximum
drawdown at most the existing 25% historical selection threshold. This threshold
is not a future loss guarantee. Both roles require supported native constraint
audits. Sparse, incompatible or failed evidence cannot qualify a changed setup;
the controller retains the unchanged reference and records the reason.

The masks are diagnostic stress tests of settings already fitted on development.
Passing is not independent out-of-sample evidence and does not correct adaptive
search bias. Existing later-window and final verification remain essential.

## Persistence, recovery and native interface

Actual challenge accounts use the existing immutable development-history store.
Each also retains a compact `research-sensitivity-account-evidence-v1` receipt
binding its plan, scenario, full evaluation basis, native support counts and
constraint audit. Resume compares loaded immutable facts with checkpoint rows;
altering a support count and recomputing only the checkpoint hash is rejected.
Completed recovery performs no additional account calculation or download.

The native controller owns initial report-to-support/audit binding; the pure
assessment verifies retained identities and comparisons. It does not reconstruct
a second order ledger or portfolio engine from account curves. Previous shards
without this optional receipt keep their exact format and hashes.

The existing collapsed **Research details** includes **Symbol and date checks**
with four compact matched-result rows. It distinguishes passed, failed,
insufficient and deliberately unperformed tests. The normal result remains one
conclusion and next action; no new setup page or indicator menu is added.
