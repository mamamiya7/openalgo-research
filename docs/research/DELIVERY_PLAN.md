## Automatic journey and product acceptance

- Import: counts, signal date span and reusable source are clear.
- Setup: capital, horizon and risk are explicit; technical search details stay optional.
- Run: reuse saved prices, obtain missing requirements once, and show actual stages.
- Answer: show selected versus original settings, later evidence and whether an
  improvement is supported. A no-improvement result remains useful.
- Explain: show where saved development trades performed better/worse, support
  counts and unavailable conditions; final observations cannot pick a new answer.
- Return: save a named setup, reopen it and reuse it without duplicate work.

## Explicitly outside this active plan

Do not expand allocation/rebalancing policies, volume/intraday discovery, learned
regimes, macro feeds, live resizing/kill switches, scanner-rule reconstruction,
OpenScript parity, Nautilus automatic parity or more asset classes. Do not add
larger calibration campaigns, new confidence scores or exhaustive-search claims.
These require a separate demonstrated use case; they are not implicit next steps.
Keep the existing optional connectors and safeguards working.

## Focused verification and batching rules

- Test changed outcomes: correct cohort math/support, unknown/sparse evidence,
  development/final separation, unchanged recommendations and old-report fallback.
- Run existing affected tests and type checks. New accounting or causal behavior
  needs its numerical checks; presentation-only changes do not repeat native
  optimization programmes or the full installation matrix.
- No new statistical claim is made by this reporting feature, so no new seed or
  false-discovery calibration campaign is required.
- Keep calculations bounded and inspect changed resource ownership using fd-audit.
- No live broker orders, credentials copied into development, unsolicited jobs,
  dependency churn or schema migration for a report-only addition.
- Keep checkpoints in ignored `.agent-native/`. Installations, pushes and release

### Search policy and what the user receives

Keep every saved recipe's limits unchanged, including v15. Compile new versions with explicit total
limits for acquisition, feature memory, proposals, native calculations, validation,
statistical analysis, storage and wall time. Reserve final verification capacity
before exploring. More families must share a measured budget; expansion cannot
silently consume all validation calls. Reject impossible domains before simulation,
reuse duplicate identities and stop at the actual bound. Early elimination uses
development evidence only and must demonstrate that its approximations do not
discard known winners unfairly. Search more deeply only through an explicit new
run/continuation that preserves exposed-period history.

Use few competing objectives. User risk limits are constraints; return, drawdown,
turnover and robustness are recorded dimensions. An optional small Pareto set shows
alternatives where improving one dimension sacrifices another, with a predeclared
default selection. Optuna provides constrained and multi-objective search, but the
application must enforce hard feasibility and test the chosen policy against the
pinned version. [Optuna multi-objective documentation](https://optuna.readthedocs.io/en/stable/tutorial/20_recipes/002_multi_objective.html)

Findings must contain the unchanged comparison, strongest feasible tested settings,
development and later evidence, important trade-offs, conditions of strength/weakness,
cost sensitivity, coverage and search scope. Explain what changed with paired
ablations where admitted; parameter importance is not proof of causation. The main
screen gives one conclusion and next action; full trials, statistics and assumptions
stay available to a professional trader without becoming the default reading task.

Positive-only Chartink CSVs supply observed triggers, not all non-trigger dates,
historical universe membership or the scanner's full formula. Optimisation can
improve management and add conditions to these captured opportunities. Discovering
or reproducing the original scanner requires package 7's complete source contract.
Repainting or revised historical captures retain that limitation in their evidence.

### Statistical diagnostics and their prerequisites

Add paired block resampling of comparable development returns, sensitivity to dates
and symbols, and execution/cost perturbations first. Blocks preserve declared temporal
and cross-symbol dependence; independently shuffling closed trades does not reproduce
a shared-cash account. Resampled curves are diagnostic estimates, not new executable
trade histories. Re-run actual simulation when the perturbation changes execution.

Probabilistic/deflated Sharpe, minimum track-record assessments and probability of
backtest overfitting are later admitted diagnostics. They require adequate observations,
search-history assumptions and compatible candidate evidence. A complete matrix is
necessary for some analyses, but not sufficient to validate adaptive selection or
independent-trial estimates. Diagnostic-specific cohorts/protocols and calibrated
fixtures are required. Never apply a paper's formula to a winners-only matrix or
display an unsupported certainty percentage. These methods address selection bias;
they do not prove optimal future performance.
[Deflated Sharpe paper](https://www.davidhbailey.com/dhbpapers/deflated-sharpe.pdf),
[Probability of backtest overfitting paper](https://www.davidhbailey.com/dhbpapers/backtest-prob.pdf).

### Automatic research: make the intelligence explicit

Reuse the current planner, hypothesis registry and context preparer. Their next
increment begins with the following prerequisite.

**Resolve the warmup mismatch first:** v4 retains100 sessions and validates equal
stock/index calendars; the current market-regime classifier needs150 prior closes
and its acquisition service requests160. Introduce a new context version with
role-specific calendar views or a separate retained market-reference window.
Acquire the shared index once for the union of those requirements; do not extend
every stock by60 sessions or alter the v4 schema/math in place. Test causal prefix
invariance, missing sessions and saved v2/v3/v4 identity before adding joint gates.

The next version must:

1. Inspect actual CSV timing, asset/venue, holding rules, selected engine,
   complete permitted parameter ranges and retained risk/cost assumptions.
2. Admit methods against observed data and financial semantics. Resolve warmup,
   calendar, benchmark and volume requirements before the price pass; then read
   Historify, acquire only missing coverage through the connected broker and
   freeze the input once. Calendar/year/special-session gaps name the actual
   date and supported range. Broader calendar admission preserves admin edits.
3. Compute pre-entry causal stock and market features once, with bounded caches.
   Stock/index dates must align. Unknown/gapped features never pass a gate;
   the current bar's completed close cannot qualify an earlier entry.
4. Test the unchanged reference, balanced applicable families and a bounded
   set of stock/market conjunctions; refine useful neighborhoods through real
   Optuna proposals. Avoid an enormous Cartesian product of every setting.
5. Challenge candidates on development windows, costs, slippage and neighboring
   settings. Select before the reserved final period; the final period confirms
   or weakens evidence and cannot rerank the search. Record all attempts and
   previously viewed periods so repeated research cannot relabel them unseen.
6. Return supported settings and scenario, meaningful improvement vs reference,
   uncertainty, stability, exclusions and a clear next action. Original settings
   and “no supported improvement” are valid outcomes. More trials alone do not
   justify stronger recommendation wording.

Retain date-aligned account return series per evaluated candidate, under explicit
storage/worker bounds, before adding study-wide multiplicity, dependence-aware
resampling, PBO or deflated-Sharpe diagnostics. Current evaluation caches contain
summaries and entry dates, not the needed complete candidate-by-time matrix.
Support counters are not statistical confidence. Include no-edge, sparse-date,
correlated-symbol, missing-history and shifting-market fixtures; measure false
discoveries and selection stability rather than only job completion.

Market trend and volatility are separate dimensions: a trending market can also
have high volatility. Start with existing causal benchmark evidence, not an
unvalidated “crisis classifier” assembled from unavailable macro streams.
Later volume/liquidity/efficiency and multi-timeframe methods require their own
data/evaluator tests. Hurst, credit spreads, volatility term structures, Kelly
resizing, PBO/DSR and bootstrap methods remain admission candidates rather than
automatic requirements; use only when their assumptions and sample size fit.
There is no promise of a global optimum, profitability or a reliable forecast.

Finish the existing long NSE daily, timed intraday and mixed-portfolio journey
first. Intraday execution currently using prior daily stock conditions is not
intraday indicator discovery. Then qualify one new asset/venue profile at a time
with actual historical contracts, calendars, expiries/rolls, fees and required
margin/borrow/corporate-action inputs. Current option-chain/Greeks/OI quotes do
not establish historical option or volatility evidence. Nautilus remains a
distinct optional runtime with explicit trailing/slippage limitations; selecting
another engine must not change semantics silently or favor whichever P&L is best.
Shadow monitoring of a chosen setup and review of fresh evidence can follow the
retained lineage. Automatic position resizing or crisis kill-switch execution
needs its own paper/live execution, risk and authorization acceptance.

## 1. Delivery decisions

1. **Finish one whole research loop before widening the market menu.** Make the current daily/intraday cash-equity workflow complete first. Other instrument families are explicit work packages with the same end-to-end acceptance, not forgotten scope.
2. **Make the Library and Experiment the organizing layer.** Backtests, actual Optuna studies, candidates, comparisons and decisions belong to an experiment. Preserve existing run/evidence identities below it.
3. **Reuse native OpenAlgo and engine features.** OpenAlgo supplies account, broker services and Historify; VectorBT/Nautilus calculate; Optuna searches and supplies analysis. Our work is requirements translation, adapters, persistence, orchestration and native presentation.
4. **Select data from the entire accepted request.** Use signal timing, execution rules, lookbacks, all allowed search values, contract history and engine requirements. Reuse matching native data, fetch missing coverage once, freeze inputs, then calculate. Material unresolved intent is a specific question; it is not guessed into a different financial experiment.
5. **Retain exact history while enabling change.** A view change stays a view change. Edited assumptions create a child version. A new data download creates new evidence. An unchanged completed study can receive another recorded execution budget through Add trials; calculation-stage Pause/Resume retains exact committed work.
6. **Keep healthy UI quiet.** Relevant fields, one primary action per context, focused result tabs and contextual drawers. No new dashboard of every diagnostic, metric or engine option.
7. **Validate the claimed combination.** Asset, venue, broker/history capability, engine, runtime and interval all contribute to support. A supported library or installed broker plugin alone does not establish that combination.
8. **Release in usable increments.** The first complete public research milestone covers the existing cash-equity scope. Advanced research, each asset family and ongoing review follow as named increments through the same release checks.

No reliable full-product calendar date is assigned before the runtime baseline and the first new asset data probe. Work below is ordered and bounded by demonstrable outcomes; unsupported historical data and access to another broker account are explicit dependencies, not hidden engineering estimates.
