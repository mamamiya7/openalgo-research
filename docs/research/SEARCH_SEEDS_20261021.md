## Frozen selection rule

The mandate records `research-search-seed-plan-v1` before scores exist. Seed 0
remains the primary final development fit. Only after chronological checks,
higher costs, paired daily ranges, neighbours, native constraint audits and
symbol/date challenges qualify, repeat the complete 68-proposal training search
with seeds 1 and 2. All three use the same original development prices, signal
pool, causal admission, parameter domains, capital, costs and hard constraints.

Each search has at most 50 identical predeclared screening proposals and at least
18 adaptive refinements. The compiler freezes the exact count from admitted
clock choices before outcomes; daily runs can have fewer clock probes and more
refinements within the same 68 proposals. Matching settings/conditions/period/cost accounts reuse the same
immutable native calculation and history, including across seeds. The repeats
do not download data. They do not repeat earlier expanding-prefix folds or their
original-sizing controls. They assess the final training search's sensitivity,
not seed stability of the entire chronological refitting procedure.

All three selected accounts need at least 60 development sessions, five completed
trades on five entry dates, supported native constraint evidence, positive net
return, drawdown at most 25%, positive return-minus-drawdown advantage against
the unchanged reference, and positive paired daily lower ranges at both existing
5- and 10-session block lengths. The smallest risk-adjusted advantage must be at
least half the largest. This fixed engineering tolerance is not a statistical
confidence threshold. The range estimator remains conditional and descriptive.

Repeat winners can only veto the primary setup. They cannot replace it with the
luckiest seed, enlarge its risk, tune a threshold, read final results or relabel
an unsupported setup. Missing, weak or inconsistent evidence keeps the unchanged
reference. Earlier failure records `not_run` and skips the additional searches.

The maximum programme is 408 proposals across four original fits plus two
repeats, within 512 calculation attempts. The conservative worst-case bound is
509, including all 64 interrupted-attempt reservations and eight symbol/date
accounts. Shared screening accounts usually make the actual increment much
smaller than the 136-account reserve. Existing total mark/byte retention limits,
8 MiB checkpoint limit, two-window cache and prepared gate lifecycle remain.

## Controlled false-recommendation calibration

The [offline protocol](SEARCH_CALIBRATION_PROTOCOL.md) freezes full procedure,
code/runner identities, independent outcome seeds and all cases before results.
The suite invokes the real v15 controller, actual Optuna, native causal gates and
verified full development shards. It uses an explicitly controlled account
outcome model and a stubbed constraint audit; genuine native execution is tested
separately. The model's fixed price/admission fixture is shared, so independence
concerns generated potential account outcomes conditional on that fixture.

Null strata give every economic setting the same expected daily return:
independent setting innovations, 85% common account shocks, a volatility shift,
and sparse admitted opportunities. Cost stress preserves each potential outcome
path and changes only costs. Numerically identical settings can share outcomes
across conditions; trial proposals are never treated as independent observations.
A persistent-edge positive control checks that an always-reference implementation
cannot pass. Deliberately adverse final outcomes cannot change the frozen choice.

Every declared run, including failures, remains in the calibration receipt.
Counts describe changed **development recommendations**, regardless of final
report status. Errors or incomplete evidence invalidate coverage. The acceptance
rule requires zero false recommendations in the declared null cases and at least
one positive-control recommendation. No threshold is retuned to the receipt.

Per-model exact binomial upper bounds describe only independent generated
datasets under that specified model. A small zero-event suite cannot establish
a 5% false-recommendation guarantee; distinct models are not pooled into one
universal bound. These results are not attached to a live study as a p-value,
probability of profit, proof of a universal optimum or general FDR control.
