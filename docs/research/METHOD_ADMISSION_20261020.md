# Method admission foundation — 20 October 2026

## Implemented scope

Package 3 starts with a typed explanatory registry and a read-only account of
the work actually recorded by the existing v8 programme. This is the foundation
for expanding methods, not a new search recipe or an executable admission engine.
The numerical catalogue remains 92 stock/market choices across 32 families;
four independent fits retain 48 proposals each and the 320-calculation ceiling.
Older recipe, mandate, checkpoint and report identities remain unchanged.

The registry pins the existing catalogue hash and defines formulas, requirements,
warmup, causal availability, missingness, comparison rules, registered domains and
supported engine/profile. Market regime thresholds are inclusive and use their
native percentile tie policy; stock comparisons retain their strict equality
guard. Daily stock features can support daily or minute execution. This does not
claim minute indicator discovery. Volume/liquidity, minute indicators and learned
models remain explicitly unsupported by this registry.

## User journey

Within an automatic v8 result, open **Research details → Methods considered**.
The main conclusion and the usual Optimize action stay unchanged. Opening the
outer details alone performs no new request. The optional view shows:

- Tested training accounts, reused proposals and registered conditions not tried.
- All fits together, or each of the three earlier fits and the final development fit.
- One selected family's formula, input requirements and missing-data policy.
- A quiet indication when recorded entry checks lacked feature inputs.

The view reads one owned, sealed calculation checkpoint on demand. It does not
load candles or full account-history shards, download prices, rerun an engine or
query Optuna. Closing it cancels the request and discards its query cache. A
checkpoint/publication change during the read asks for a reload. Legacy runs and
unverifiable records report unavailable details without changing saved results.

## Meaning of coverage

Tested counts represent distinct proposal-linked training accounts, including
different settings within a family. They do not count validation passes, prove a
family works, or establish a global optimum. Combined counts add independent fits;
the same condition may be tested in several fits. Not-tried counts are unique
registered condition identities absent from the proposals in that selected scope.
Only a finished fit can mark untried conditions budget-deferred; unfinished work
remains pending. An evaluated family can still contain deferred conditions.

The builder reconciles mandate, pinned catalogue, proposal settings, evaluation
receipts and the development-history index. This is metadata consistency, not
numerical replay, shard-existence verification or complete price coverage.
Unknown-entry counts can include repeated checks of the same missing observation;
they are never presented as unique missing symbols or candles. Clock identifiers
and materialised settings are checked, but the compact metadata cannot re-certify
membership of the original calendar's clock grid. Full numerical recovery retains
its existing stronger checks.

## Next implementation dependency

Expand one qualified price family with a new numerical recipe/catalogue version,
then reserve family screening, refinement and validation work before enabling it.
The first candidate is absolute momentum over 10 and 60 completed sessions at
registered zero and 2% thresholds. It fits the existing stock warmup; wider EMA
domains need their own larger warmup admission. ATR-scaled protective exits need
native same-bar execution tests and are a later slice. None of these additional
methods is implemented or advertised as tested by this increment.

Preserve v8 replay unchanged. A new programme must define its proposal allocation,
coverage, recovery and evidence contracts before making expanded choices the
default. No trial may acquire data independently. Portfolio constraints, richer
robust selection and adaptive regime policies follow the delivery plan.
